What was promised. What arrived. And the receipt for every number.

Launching this fall

A data center is being built in LaPorte. Promises were made — about jobs, about taxes, about water and electricity. Some of those promises are already being kept. Some cannot be measured yet. This site will track all of them in one place, and show you the source document behind every figure.

We are not for the project or against it. We are for the record.

The tracker

Every public commitment becomes a line you can watch fill in over time — the way a fundraising thermometer works. Below is how it will look. These are the categories we will track, not results: nothing has been published yet.

Property taxes to LaPorte schoolsAgreement in place
A share of the project's property-tax revenue is committed to the school corporation. Revenue is expected to begin in 2028 — so this line stays honest and mostly empty until money actually moves.
Permanent jobsNot yet measurable
Construction jobs and permanent jobs are different things, and they are often blurred together. We will count them separately, against state employment data rather than announcements.
Water use and replenishmentNo independent measurement exists
Public estimates for this site currently differ by a factor of hundreds. Until someone publishes a real figure, this line stays at zero and says so.
Electricity — impact on residential billsNo independent measurement exists
The most common question we hear. It will be answered from utility filings, not from press releases — and a widely-shared figure about Indiana data center power turns out to belong to a different company entirely.
Local hiringAwaiting a defined measure
A commitment to hire locally is only checkable once "local" has a definition and a number attached. Asking for that is our first question, not an accusation.

Some lines will sit empty for a long time. That is the honest state of a project that broke ground in 2026 and opens in 2029 — and a tracker that showed otherwise would be lying to you.

How we work

Every number has a receipt. Click any figure and you will see the document and page — or the recording and timestamp — it came from. If we cannot show you the source, we do not print the number.
We save the source before we read it. Every document and recording is preserved and fingerprinted the moment we obtain it. If a page is later edited or taken down, we can still prove what it said.
We check the good news too. When a commitment is kept, that is a headline here. When a widely-shared claim against the project turns out to be wrong, we say so. A tracker that only ever reports failures is a campaign, not a record.
Nobody is quoted unless we know who spoke. In a room full of officials, company representatives and residents, it is easy for a machine to put one person's words in another person's mouth. We separate and confirm every speaker first. When we cannot, we do not attribute.
We ask before we publish. Anyone named in a finding is offered a real chance to respond, with a deadline, and their answer runs alongside it. This is built into our system: a finding without a logged response attempt cannot be published.
We correct in the open. When we get something wrong, the correction stays visible along with what it replaced. We have already corrected ourselves once, publicly, in our own working files.

What this site will not do

  • No comment sections, no ratings, no scoring companies or officials.
  • No opinion columns and no endorsements — for the project or against it.
  • No publishing anonymous claims. Submit evidence; we will verify it or we will not run it.
  • No advertiser, sponsor or partner has any say over a single figure on this site. Ever. We will publish who funds us.

Have something we should see?

Send us a document

Meeting recordings, letters, notices, permits, utility bills, photographs. We will check whether it is authentic before it goes anywhere near the site.

Ask us a question

The questions residents actually ask are what we research first — water, electricity, zoning, jobs, and what happens if the company leaves.

Submissions open at launch

We are preparing the intake process now, including how we verify what is sent to us and how we protect people who send it. Anything submitted is reviewed before publication — never posted automatically.
