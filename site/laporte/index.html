Why we built this

Nobody was arguing about facts.
They were arguing about numbers nobody could check.

A billion-dollar data center is going up in LaPorte. Residents started asking exactly the right questions — about water, about electricity bills, about jobs, about what the county actually agreed to.

The answers they got came from press releases, from opponents, and from social media. Almost none of it could be traced back to a source. And some of the numbers circulating most widely in Indiana turn out, on inspection, to describe an entirely different company's project.

Meanwhile the state's own economic development leadership said publicly, in August 2026, that the message about growth "is not landing." That is not a messaging problem. It is a trust problem — and more messaging has never once solved one.

So we built the other thing. Not another voice in the argument: the shared record underneath it, where anyone — supporter, skeptic, reporter, or the company itself — can click a number and see exactly where it came from.

The tracker

Every promise becomes a line that fills in

Like a fundraising thermometer: it starts empty and climbs as commitments are independently confirmed. Below are the categories we will track. These are not results — nothing has been published yet.

Property taxes to LaPorte schoolsAgreement in place
A share of the project's property-tax revenue is committed to the school corporation for 20 years. Revenue is expected to begin in 2028 — so this line stays mostly empty until money actually moves.
Permanent jobsNot yet measurable
Construction jobs and permanent jobs are different things and are frequently blurred together. We count them separately, against state employment data rather than announcements.
Water use and replenishmentNo independent measurement exists
Public estimates for this site currently differ by a factor of hundreds. Until a real figure is published, this line stays at zero and says so.
Electricity — impact on residential billsNo independent measurement exists
The most common question we hear. It will be answered from utility regulatory filings, not from either side's characterization of them.
Local hiringAwaiting a defined measure
A commitment to hire locally becomes checkable once "local" has a definition and a number attached. Asking for that is our first question — not an accusation.

Three of five lines are empty. That is the point.

This project broke ground in 2026 and opens in 2029. A tracker showing confident numbers today would be inventing them. Where no independent measurement exists, we say so and leave the bar at zero — and we name who would have to publish the figure for that to change.

How it works

Evidence, sealed before it is read

This site runs on PrecognitionOS, a governed AI system built to produce findings that survive being challenged — by a resident, a reporter, a county attorney, or a trillion-dollar company's lawyer.

Every number has a receipt. Click any figure and you get the document and page — or the recording and timestamp — it came from. If we can't show the source, we don't print the number.
Sources are preserved before analysis. Every document and recording is fingerprinted and sealed the moment we obtain it. If a page is later edited or deleted, we can still prove what it said.
Figures are never summarized by AI. Numbers are extracted mechanically from the sealed source. We have watched AI summarizers invent three different budget figures from a single article — so ours are not permitted to touch them.
Nobody is quoted unless we know who spoke. In a room of officials, company representatives and residents, it is trivially easy for software to put one person's words in another's mouth. We separate and confirm every speaker first. When we can't, we don't attribute.
We look for the case against our own finding. Before anything is recorded, the system searches for the strongest evidence that we are wrong. Findings that don't survive that don't run.
We ask before we publish. Anyone named is offered a real chance to respond, with a deadline, and their answer runs alongside the finding. This is enforced in the software: a finding with no logged response attempt cannot be published.
We check the good news too. A kept commitment is a headline here. A widely-shared claim against the project that turns out to be false gets corrected here. A tracker that only reports failures is a campaign, not a record.

What this technology is, plainly

PrecognitionOS is CyberHopeAI's evidence-chain platform — the same system used for regulated, audit-grade work where an AI's conclusion has to be defensible months later. It preserves the original source, tracks who said what and when, weighs conflicting evidence, and seals the whole chain so any finding can be re-examined and reproduced.

This site is a public demonstration of it. We think showing the work in a community that genuinely needs it beats any advertisement we could buy.

What this site will never do

  • No comment sections, no ratings, no scoring of companies or officials.
  • No opinion columns and no endorsements — for the project or against it.
  • No publishing of anonymous claims. Submit evidence; we verify it or we don't run it.
  • No political advocacy of any kind. We are for the record, not for a side.
