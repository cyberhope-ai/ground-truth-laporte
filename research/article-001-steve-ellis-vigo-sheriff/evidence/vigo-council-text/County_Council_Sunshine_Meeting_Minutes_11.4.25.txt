VIGO COUNTY COUNCIL
Sunshine Meeting Minutes
Tuesday, November 4, 2025 at 5:00 P.M.
Council Chambers, Vigo County Government Center

Pledge of Allegiance

Brad Anderson called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m.
Calling of the Roll

Nancy Allsup — present; Vicki Weger — present; Steve Ellis — present; Randy Gentry — absent; Brenda
Wilson — present; Brad Anderson — present; and David Thompson — present.
Communications from elected officials, other officials, or agencies of the County
Councilman Steve Ellis read a statement from councilperson Randy Gentry:
“With a pending court hearing scheduled for 11/14 — a court hearing at my request — I continue to fear for the
safety of my family and me 24 hours a day seven days a week, While the sheriff department and Sheriff Fell
have been kind in offering additional for security for me during any meeting I attend, the taxpayer cost of such a
request for a non-voting Council Sunshine Meeting is not justified. Even with additional security, I am not sure
I would be comfortable to attend. I am notifying you that I will not be able to be at the sunshine meeting this
evening, but will actively watch the hearing live. After the threats that were made against me after the last
meeting by the Council president, my personal safety must take precedent. Violence and threats of violence are
never acceptable, but one council member threatening another is beyond reasonable. All Council members have
to feel safe to speak, make decisions, and discuss matters without fear of violence or retribution. I thank
everyone for their understanding during this difficult time. My best, Randy Gentry.”
Chief Deputy Prosecutor Rob Roberts approached the Council and asked for clarifications on errors to the
proposed Prosecutor’s and Public Defender’s Salary Ordinance. He said he hoped to be able to ask questions at
an upcoming Annual Budget Committee meeting.
Commissioner Mike Morris approached the Council and noted Hasselburger Bridge was scheduled for
completion the following week. He said Culvert, chip and seal, and paving projects for the year were nearly
complete. He also said the County was awarded a grant from the DNR for the Riley Trail.
Reports from Committees
There were none.
Resolutions and Ordinances Other than Appropriations — First Reading

a. Approving Seelyville 2026 Civil Town Budget

| Council Attorney Michael Wright explained this vote would approve the Seelyville Civil Town Budget
Approval. He said the DLGF had contacted the Auditor’s Office and said due to a conflict of interest with two

firemen on the Seelyville Council, the Vigo County Council needed to approve Seelyville’s approval of their
Budget as another layer of accountability. Brad Anderson made a motion to approve Seelyville’s Civil Town
Budget. Vicki Weger seconded the motion. The motion passed with a 6-0 vocal vote.

b. Ordinance Adopting 2026 County of Vigo Indiana Salary Schedule and Compensation Policies
Michael Wright explained these salary ordinances were the product of the work of the 2026 Council Annual
Budget Committee. He said this general employee ordinance reflected the changes to compensation agreed
upon by the Annual Budget Committee. There were no questions.

c. Ordinance Adopting 2026 County of Vigo, Indiana Salary Schedule and Compensation
Policies of Merit Officers
Michael said Sheriff Fell and his administration had met with the Annual Budget Committee and this ordinance
reflected the new dates and compensation rates agreed upon by the Annual Budget Committee and there were
no substantial changes beyond this. There were no questions.
d. Ordinance Adopting 2026 County of Vigo, Indiana Salary Schedule and Compensation
Policies of Prosecutors and Public Defenders
Michael addressed the comments Rob Roberts made earlier in the meeting. Michael said he had helped prepare
these ordinances, and Rob’s concerns about the amount and nature of the budgeted deputies had been a simple
mistake on his own part. There were no further questions.
e. Ordinance Adopting 2026 County of Vigo, Indiana Salary Schedule and Compensation
Policies of the Highway Department
Michael noted the dates on this ordinance were adjusted to reflect the new year and the charts had been updated
with the proposed compensation rates for 2026. Michael said beyond the standard changes, there were no
substantial adjustments. There were no questions.
f. Ordinance Adopting 2026 County of Vigo, Indiana Salary Schedule and Compensation
Policies of Dispatch/E911
Michael noted there was additional language inserted into this ordinance adding a probationary period for
people coming into the dispatch position giving the department head more discretion in terms of gauging a
person’s ability to perform the job. He said beyond this the normal modifications were made. There were no
questions.
g. Ordinance Adopting 2026 County of Vigo, Indiana Salary Schedule and Compensation
Policies of Correctional/Detention/Building Security Officers
Michael said the normal modifications to this ordinance were made adding the compensation rates agreed upon
by the annual budget committee. There were no questions.
h. Ordinance Adopting 2026 County of Vigo, Indiana Salary Schedule and Compensation
Policies of the Parks Department
Michael noted this ordinance reflected the new proposed part-time employee compensation rate agreed upon by
the Annual Budget Committee. He said the normal modifications were made to this ordinance adding new
compensations rates agreed upon by the Committee. There were no questions.
ji. Ordinance Adopting 2026 County of Vigo, Indiana Salary Schedule and Compensation
Policies of the Health Department
Michael said the normal modifications were made to this ordinance for 2026 and it now reflected compensation
rates decided upon by the Annual Budget Committee.
j. Ordinance Adopting 2026 County of Vigo, Indiana Salary Schedule and Compensation
Policies of Elected Officials
Michael explained this ordinance contained the annual compensation for 2026 for Vigo County’s Elected
Officials. There were no questions.
k. Ordinance Adopting 2026 County of Vigo, Indiana Salary Schedule and Compensation
Policies of Court Employees
Michael said this proposed ordinance had been provided to the Council before this meeting. Michael noted this
was a new ordinance not adopted in previous years, due to the settlement agreement made between the Council

Brad Anderson cited the judge’s mandate, retirement increases for the sheriffs department, increasing

1. Resolution of Re-Allocation of Existing Appropriation 2025-8: Adult Probation — Commercial
Shredder Purchase
Chief Probation Officer Lee Ann Reisenbeck explained her request to transfer $5,200 to the New Equipment
line item from the Travel Expenses, Mileage, Registration, and Service Agreements, line items to purchase
commercial shredder for the Adult Probation Department. Lee Ann explained they had lost access to the
shredder from Community Corrections, and the department handles confidential documents. Brad Anderson
asked if this would be a lease or purchase. Lee Ann confirmed this would be a purchase. There were no further
questions.
Ordinances Related to Appropriations — First Reading
a. Additional Appropriation 2025-54: Sheriff's Office — Jail Medical Costs
Sheriff Derek Fell approached the Council and explained this request for $190,000 to be appropriated would
cover the remainder of the jail medical costs for the remainder of the year. Derek said the remaining cost before
the end of the year had actually risen to $315,000 since this original request. Brad Anderson asked if the amount
the Council could appropriate could be increased. Michael said due to the proposed additional appropriation
being advertised out of three different funds — County General, LIT Correctional Rehab Facilities, and Juvenile
Justice Center Non-Reverting — the Council could increase the amount appropriated based on the Sheriff's
request, the Council would just need to appropriate some of the request from one of the other funds in the
advertisement. Brenda Wilson asked if this bill could be encumbered if necessary. Michael confirmed this was
possible. There were no further questions.
b. Additional Appropriation 2025-55: Vigo County Engineering — Road Salt & Equipment
Repair
Larry Robbins approached the Council and explained his request. He said he would like the Council to
appropriate $140,000 for Road Salt, and $50,000 for equipment repair. Larry said he could decrease his budget
by the requested amount in 2026 to offset the expense this year. Larry noted he usually orders salt in January,
but reserves were low ahead of the winter season. Brad Anderson asked if the price of road salt had gone up.
Larry said the price had increased by a small amount. Larry said this year had been rough for equipment
breaking down, and the $50,000 would go toward repairing equipment for the remainder of the year.
| c. Additional Appropriation 2025-56: Commissioners — Casino/Riverboat Wagering Revenue
Request
Commissioner Mark Clinkenbeard and Kristin Craig from the Terre Haute Chamber of Commerce gave a brief
presentation to the Council detailing the reasoning and history of the Commissioners request for $100,000 to be
appropriated from the Riverboat Wagering Tax Revenue Fund to create an oversight committee to find funding
solutions for the Vigo County School Corporation’s Modernization and Consolidation plan. Attorney Chou-il
Lee approached the Council and explained the parameters and limitations of House Bill 1515. Steve Ellis and
Chou-il had an extended discussion about their respective interpretations of the law. Chou-il explained how
Indiana state statutes are read and said the oversight committee cannot expend funds not appropriated by
Council. Steve expressed his concerns the committee would spend funds beyond what was expected leaving the
County responsible for these costs. Steve voiced his opinion the Council should not appropriate funds to this
oversight board. Commissioner Chris Switzer voiced his desire for Vigo County to utilize this legislation to

===== [County_Council_Sunshine_Meeting_Minutes_11.4.25] PAGE 4 =====
improve area schools. Steve expressed he believed the discussed law was poorly written. Chou-il explained the
law only in effect when the County gifts money to the school corporation and the committee would then be
subject to open door laws. Steve once again voiced his disagreement with this process and said he would prefer
a committee of private citizens form their own committee. All three Commissioners approached the council and
said they all were requesting this appropriation in unison and were asking for the Council’s help to get the
process of an oversight committee. Mark Clinkenbeard and Mike Morris expressed their disagreement with
Steve Ellis’ objections to this process and request. Chris Switzer once again voiced his desire to utilize House
Bill 1515 for Vigo County and confusion on why the Council was resistant to the process. Chris cited the large
amount of support the Bill had at the Indiana Statehouse. Chris Switzer requested Council members with
questions approach them in the days following the meeting. David Thompson asked School Superintendent Dr.
Chris Himsel what the School Corporation would do if the oversight committee was not formed. Himsel said
the School Corporation would reevaluate costs and proceed with the modernization and consolidation plan.
Himsel briefly explained the state of the schools and history of attempts to correct issues at the buildings and
why the Board and the Corporation had determined this plan was necessary.
