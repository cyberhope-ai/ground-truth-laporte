VIGO COUNTY COUNCIL
Sunshine Meeting Minutes
Tuesday, June 2, 2026 at 5:00 P.M.
Council Chambers, Vigo County Government Center

Pledge of Allegiance

Steve Ellis called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m.
Calling of the Roll

Brenda Wilson — present; David Thompson — present; Nancy Allsup — present; Brad
Anderson — present; Randy Gentry — present; Vicki Weger — present; Steve Ellis — present.
Communications from elected officials, other officials, or agencies of the County
Terre Haute Mayor Brandon Sakbun approached the Council noted there had been efforts made
by city officials to determine what amount of opioid settlement funds the city was willing to
offer to programming and support of the judicial system.

a. Vigo County School Oversight Committee — June 2026 Report
Council appointed member of the Vigo County School Oversight Committee Heather Pickens
approached the Council and provided a brief report of the Committee’s June activities. Heather
said there were no new contracts to report since the last meeting, the Committee had not entered
into any new service agreements. Heather said the Committee had received one invoice from
Barnes & Thornburg for services rendered through April 30, 2026. She said the Committee’s
expenses up to the date of this meeting were right under $10,000. She said the Committee was on
schedule to provide a recommendation on the Vigo County School Consolidation and
Modernization Plan by Mid-July.
Executive Director of Vigo County Public Libraries Jordan Orwig provided a brief report of
services and programs provided by the library system in 2025.
Auditor Larry Hutchings provided a brief update on the rollout of the digital claims system for
the County.
Reports from Committees
There were none.
Resolutions and Ordinances Other than Appropriations — First Reading

a. 2026 Tax Abatement Compliance Review
The Council was provided documentation for all active Vigo County Tax Abatements for their
review. Council Administrator Devan Gackle explained the Council would be responsible for
finding if those granted an active abatement were in substantial compliance the following
meeting.
Ordinances Related to Appropriations — First Reading

a. Additional Appropriation 2026-29: Community Corrections — Budget
Correction
Director of Court Services Bill Watson was not able to be present at this meeting. Deputy
Auditor Judy DeLisle explained this request was for $200,000 to be appropriated from the LIT-
Correctional Rehab Fund to the Supplement to Community Correction. Judy said this
appropriation was reduced during the budgeting process the previous year, but Bill Watson said
this reduction in the budget was a mistake and was requesting the restoration of this part the
budget to the level it was during previous years. There was discussion amongst the Council about
whether or not this cut was intentional.
b. Additional Appropriation 2026-30: Jail — Utility Costs
Judy DeLisle approached the Council and explained this request for $12,000 to be appropriated
from the LIT — Correctional Rehab Fund to the Contractual Services line in the Fund in order to
pay the Jail’s Dish Bill. Judy said these funds should have been budgeted during the annual
budgeting process, but were not due to an oversight.
c. Additional Appropriation 2026-31: Group Homes — Non-Reverting Fund
Request
Group Homes Executive Director Norm Loudermilk approached the Council and explained this
request for $50,000 to be appropriated from the Group Homes Non-Reverting Fund to the
Institutional Supplies line, $64,000 from the same fund to Contractual Services line, and $6,000
from the same fund to the New Equipment line would cover expenses for the rest of the year and
reduce the amount of request the department would need to make before the Council. There was
discussion on the appropriations process and the responsibilities of the Council versus the
Commissioners. There was discussion on the income of the Group Homes from the DCS’s
ability to support both County Group Homes. Norm noted incoming DCS money was down to
due to cuts at the State level, but the income nearly covered the department’s expenses. There
was discussion on getting the word out the necessary agencies Vigo County Group Homes were
operational an accepting children. Commissioner Mike Morris approached the Council and
expressed his support for Group Homes and noted this request would simply the project funding
for the department.
d. Additional Appropriation 2026-32: Commissioners — Liability Claims and
Worker’s Comp. Costs
Vigo County Commissioner Chris Switzer approached the Council and explained this request
was for $500,000 to be appropriated to the Liability Claims line in the LIT-Correctional Rehab
Fund and $117,178 to the Workman’s Compensation Line in the County General Fund. He said
the appropriation from the LIT Correctional Rehab fund was due to significant lawsuits
underway at the County Jail. He noted due to the slow nature of legal processes, the
appropriation granted last year for this purpose reverted back to the General Fund unused. Chris
explained the role of the Commissioners in this process versus the role of the elected Sheriff in
policies and operation of the Jail. He also noted he could not go into detail about the mentioned
lawsuits due to them being ongoing cases. Chris said so far in the year the legal claims have
totaled $181,723.51. There was discussion about the amount of pending cases and the length of
time it has taken to attempt to resolve them. There was discussion on this request being from the
LIT —Correctional Rehab versus the General Fund Request for the same purpose the previous

e. Additional Appropriation 2026-33: Public Defender — Title [V E Grant Fund

Request

Public Defender Gretchen Etling approached the Council and explained this request was for
$12,024 to be appropriated to the Title [V Grant Fund in the Drug Testing line. She said would
assist with the drug screens for mental health cases in the problem solving courts. Gretchen
Etling confirmed the Fund has the amount of cash to support this requested appropriation.

f. Additional Appropriation 2026-34: Prairieton Fire Protection District — New

Apparatus

Treasurer for the Prairieton/Prairie Creek Fire Protection District Board Paul Fouts approached
the Council and explained this request for an approval of an additional appropriation of $25,000
from their Cumulative Fund and $24,000 from their General Fund. He said the Board voted to
approve the additional appropriations and they now needed final approval from the County
Council. These appropriations would be used to purchase a new apparatus for the Prairieton Fire
Protection District.
Public Comment
There were none.
Appointments
Steve Ellis said he had appointed Vicki Weger, Nancy Allsup, and himself to the Annual Budget
Committee.
Larry noted there were scheduled Budget Committee Hearings June 10%, 11, and 12.
Adjournment
Vicki Weger made a motion to adjourn the meeting. Nancy Allsup seconded the motion. The
meeting adjourned at 5:46 p.m.
