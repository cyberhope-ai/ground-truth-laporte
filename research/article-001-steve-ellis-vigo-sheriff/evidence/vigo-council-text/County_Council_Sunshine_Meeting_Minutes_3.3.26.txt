VIGO COUNTY COUNCIL
Sunshine Meeting Minutes
Tuesday, March 3, 2026 at 5:00 P.M.
Council Chambers, Vigo County Government Center
Pledge of Allegiance
Steve Ellis called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m.
Calling of the Roll
Brenda Wilson — absent; David Thompson — present; Nancy Allsup — present, Brad Anderson — absent;
Randy Gentry — present; Vicki Weger — present; Steve Ellis — present.
Communications from elected officials, other officials, or agencies of the County
Steve Ellis expressed gratitude for Nancy Allsup and Randy Gentry’s vote related to funding an unnamed
project at the previous month’s meeting.
a. Thrive West Central Update Presentation
Ryan Keller provided a presentation on Thrive West Central Operations and Vigo County development and
housing programs. He said 560 housing units had been awarded as of January 2026. He discussed goals of the
organization for 2026 and various housing programs both in progress and planned for the future. Vicki Weger
expressed her concern for food deserts in the Vigo County Area and the difficulty in some groups in
transportation to these areas. Ryan confirmed Thrive West Central was thinking about how to solicit the private
sector into investing in food markets in these areas. Ryan confirmed Thrive West Central’s core district was 7
counties and the organization does business with 15 counties in some way. He confirmed 71% of the
organization’s budget is funded from government dollars. Ryan said thanks to a grant matching request, Vigo
County contributed approximately $200,000, and in 2024 the organization received ARPA funds.
b. Hamilton Center Update
Hamilton Center Chief of Clinical Outreach Emily Owens approached the Council and provided a requested
update of the amount of certificates of completion for discharges of the center. She said about 1,200 had been
discharged due to successful treatment, and this was about 30% to 40% of patients served.
| Reports from Committees
There were none.

Resolutions and Ordinances Other than Appropriations — First Reading
a. An Amended Ordinance Adopting the 2026 Salary Schedule and Compensation Policies for
Vigo County, Indiana
Council Administrator Devan Gackle explained these proposed amendments to the sections of the salary
ordinance detailing Pre-Trial screener’s compensation, clarifications to deputy coroner’s compensation
schedule, and clarifications to election board stipends were tabled at the last Council meeting and are not before
Council to discuss at this meeting. She said these changes were requested by the Human Resources Department
| and the Council had been presented letters requesting these changes from HR.
Ordinances Related to Appropriations — First Reading
a. Additional Appropriation 2026-5: Courts — Pre-Trial Screener Salary Funding
Devan explained this Additional Appropriation of $2,448 in the “payroll salaries” line item in the Circuit Court
budget, $188 in the “FICA” line item, and $348 in the “PERF” line would fund the amendment to the Pre-Trial
screeners discussed at the February 3“ and February 10 Council meetings. Director of Court Services Bill

Watson explained there were two pre-trial screener positions funded by the State, and two of the exact same
positions funded by the County and in an effort to keep the pay lined up across the positions, the budget is
adjusted in this manner in the following year once the compensation for the two state-funded positons is known.
Bill confirmed the Annual Budget Committee was notified about this every year.
b. Additional Appropriation 2026-13: Community Corrections — Employee Benefits
A version of this request to appropriate $100,394 for “FICA,” $186,351 for “PERF,” and $407,963 for Group
Insurance to fund benefits for Community Corrections employees was presented before Council at the February
3", 2025 Sunshine meeting and the February 10", 2025 Council meeting. Randy noted the Council does not
budget Community Corrections, but the Community Corrections Board does. He requested the Community
Corrections Board make budget adjustments in the future budgeting process to assist the County in covering this
cost.
c. Additional Appropriation 2026-14: Assessor’s Office — New CAMA System
Kevin Gardner approached the Council and explained this request for $231,306 to be appropriated in “Service
Agreements” in the Reassessment Fund would pay for the transition and change of CAMA system providers.
Kevin had approached the Council, notified, and explained this request at the February 3" Sunshine Council
Meeting. Kevin noted there would not be a requirement for payment to the new provider until January 2027.
There was brief discussion on the appropriate way to appropriate these funds due to the payment not being
required until January 2027.
d. Additional Appropriation 2026-15: Prosecutor’s Office — City Hall to Courthouse Office
Relocation Costs
This request was to appropriate $1,350 in the “contractual services” line, $3,848 in the “office machines” line,
and $6,500 in the “office furniture” line in the Prosecutor’s budget in the County General Fund. There was no
representative from the Prosecutor’s office to further explain this request.
e. Additional Appropriation 2026-16: Terre Haute Mayor’s Office — Re-Appropriating
Unencumbered Funds for Warming Center
This request before Council was to re-appropriate $15,000 from the Rainy Day Fund to pay for warming center
costs. These funds were appropriated at the very end of 2025, but not encumbered into the next budget year.
Public Comment
| There was none.
Adjournment
Randy Gentry made a motion to adjourn the meeting. Vicki Weger seconded the motion. The meeting
adjourned at 5:33 p.m.
