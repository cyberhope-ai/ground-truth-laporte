Ordinances Relating to Appropriations

a. Additional Appropriation 2026-29: Community Corrections — Budget

Correction

Director of Court Services Bill Watson explained this request was for $200,000 to be
appropriated from the LIT- Correctional Rehab Fund to the Supplement to Community
Correction. Judy said this appropriation was reduced during the budgeting process the previous
year, but Bill Watson said this reduction in the budget was a mistake and was requesting the
restoration of this part the budget to the level it was during previous years. Randy asked about
Rule 10.3. Can they address this again since it’s within the 12 months since it was originally
voted on? They are planning to revisit this in the August meeting.

b. Additional Appropriation 2026-30: Jail — Utility Cost
Judy DeLisle approached the Council and explained this request for $12,000 to be appropriated
from the LIT — Correctional Rehab Fund to the Contractual Services line in the Fund in order to
pay the Jail’s Dish Bill. Judy said these funds should have been budgeted during the annual
budgeting process but were not due to an oversight. It was brought up to look at other options
that maybe don’t cost so much. Vicki Weger made a motion to approve Additional
Appropriation 2026-30. Nancy Allsup seconded the motion. Upon a roll call vote, voting was as
follows: Brenda Wilson-aye, Nancy Allsup-aye, Brad Anderson-aye, Randy Gentry-nay, Vicki
Weger-aye, Steve Ellis-aye. With a 5-1 vote the motion passes.

c. Additional Appropriation 2026-31: Group Homes-Non-Reverting Fund request
Group Homes Executive Director Norm Loudermilk approached the Council and explained this
request for $50,000 to be appropriated from the Group Homes Non-Reverting Fund to the
Institutional Supplies line, $64,000 from the same fund to Contractual Services line, and $6,000
from the same fund to the New Equipment line would cover expenses for the rest of the year and
reduce the amount of request the department would need to make before the Council. There was
discussion on the appropriations process and the responsibilities of the Council versus the
Commissioners. There was discussion on the income of the Group Homes from the DCS’s
ability to support both County Group Homes. Norm noted incoming DCS money was down to
due to cuts at the State level, but the income nearly covered the department’s expenses. Brad
Anderson asked about the guttering on Farrington Grove House. Norm did get quotes for
windows, roof, decking and guttering. Nancy Allsup made a motion to table this until August
meeting so they can review it further. Vicki Weger seconded the motion. The vote was 5-1 to
table this until the August meeting.

d. Additional Appropriation 2026-32: Commissioners-Liability Claims and
Worker’s Compensation Costs
Vigo County Commissioner Chris Switzer approached the Council and explained this request
was for $500,000 to be appropriated to the Liability Claims line in the LIT-Correctional Rehab
Fund and $117,178 to the Workman’s Compensation Line in the County General Fund. He said
the appropriation from the LIT Correctional Rehab fund was due to significant lawsuits
underway at the County Jail. He noted due to the slow nature of legal processes, the
appropriation granted last year for this purpose reverted back to the General Fund unused. Chris
explained the role of the Commissioners in this process versus the role of the elected Sheriff in
policies and operation of the Jail. He also noted he could not go into detail about the mentioned
lawsuits due to them being ongoing cases. Chris said so far in the year the legal claims have
totaled $181,723.51. There was discussion about the amount of pending cases and the length of
time it has taken to attempt to resolve them. There was discussion on this request being from the
LIT —Correctional Rehab versus the General Fund Request for the same purpose the previous
year. Chris explained the next part of this request for an appropriation to the Workmen’s
Compensation line in the General Fund was due to the Workmen’s Compensation Audit finding
there was not enough appropriated funds for the Worker’s Compensation costs in 2026 due to the
county’s salaries being raised to the Baker Tilly Midpoint during the budgeting process the
previous year. Chris said the county had continued to pay the Worker’s Compensation Insurance,
but now the Commissioners were requesting the funding required for the mandated audit.
Switzer confirmed this audit was automatic. Mike Morris noted there would be increases in
Health Insurance Premiums for the County during the annual budgeting process. Any money not
used will revert back to the other accounts. Randy Gentry made a motion to approve this.
Nancy Allsup seconded the motion. The voting was as follows: Brenda Wilson-aye, Nancy
Allsup-aye, Brad Anderson-aye, Randy Gentry- aye, Vicki Weger-aye, and Steve Ellis-aye.
With a vote of 6-0 the motion passed.
e. Additional Appropriation 2026-33: Public Defender-Title IV E Grant Fund
Request

$12,024 to be appropriated to the Title IV Grant Fund in the Drug Testing line. She said would
assist with the drug screens for mental health cases in the problem-solving courts. Gretchen
Etling confirmed the Fund has the amount of cash to support this requested appropriation. Vicki
Weger made a motion to pass this appropriation. Brenda Wilson seconded the motion. The
voting was as follows: Brenda Wilson-aye, Nancy Allsup-aye, Brad Anderson-aye, Randy
Gentry-aye, Vicki Weger-aye, Steve Ellis, aye. With a vote of 6-0 the motion passed.
