VIGO COUNTY COUNCIL
Sunshine Meeting Minutes
Wednesday, May 6, 2026 at 4:00 P.M.
Council Chambers, Vigo County Government Center

Pledge of Allegiance

Steve Ellis called the meeting to order at 4:00 p.m.
Calling of the Roll

Brenda Wilson — absent; David Thompson — present; Nancy Allsup — present, Brad Anderson — present;
Randy Gentry — present; Vicki Weger — present; Steve Ellis — present.
Communications from elected officials, other officials, or agencies of the County
Vigo County Assessor Kevin Gardner approached the Council and noted that Form 11’s had been mailed to
taxpayers. He suggested Council make a decision on whether or not they would like tax abatements be
presented to Council. He said that legislative changes had made $2 million and under in personal property
exempt. Commissioner Mike Morris then joined Kevin and they explained taxes have gone way up for people
with a disability. He said this was due to legislative changes at the state level qualifying disability as a credit
instead of a deduction as it was historically. There was discussion on relief would be coming in adjustments to
the law at the state level. Kevin noted any changes would be too late for this year. Mike said there was roughly
1,088 people in Vigo County impacted by this.

a. Vigo County School Oversight Committee - May 2026 Report
Council appointed member of the Vigo County School Oversight Committee Heather Pickens approached the
Council and provided a brief report of the Committee’s May activities. She said the Committee was having
discussion on outside organizations for possible capital support. Heather said there were no new contracts to
report since the last meeting, the Committee had not entered into any service agreements. Heather said the
Committee had received one invoice from Barnes & Thornburg for services rendered through March 31, 2026.
She said the remaining balance on the account netting interest income and this pending invoice was $93,942.42.
Reports from Committees
There were none.
Resolutions and Ordinances Other than Appropriations — First Reading

a. An Amended Ordinance Adopting the 2026 Salary Schedule and Compensation Policies for

Vigo County Prosecutors and Public Defenders: Section 4 Amendment

Council Administrator Devan Gackle explained to Council Public Defender Gretchen Etling had reached out to
her and made her aware that Section 4 of this Ordinance was missing the words “and Public Defenders” when
referring to Prosecutors and Public Defenders being eligible for longevity pay. Gretchen had noticed this
omission when working on state paperwork and this amendment would add these words. There would be no
changes to funds budgeted. There was discussion on if this error was present in the 2025 budgeting process as
well as the 2026.
Ordinances Related to Appropriations — First Reading

a. Additional Appropriation 2026-26: Prosecutor’s Office — Law Enforcement Grants
Chief Deputy Prosecutor Rob Roberts approached the C ouncil and explained his request for $25,000 to be
appropriated in the Law Enforcement Grants line in the User Fee Infraction Deferral Fund and $5,000 to be
appropriated in the Youth Crime Prevention Grants line in the same Fund. Rob said the budgeted funds were

===== [County_Council_Sunshine_Meeting_Minutes_5.6.26] PAGE 2 =====
used to assist the Sheriffs Office in purchasing a vehicle and this appropriation would replenish these funds for
the office for the rest of 2026. There was discussion about if these funds were part of a non-reverting fund. Rob
confirmed these funds would not be reverting back to the County General Fund due to them not mingling with
County General Funds in the first place. He said these funds were collected from user fees. The Auditor’s
Office confirmed the cash balance of the fund was approximately $113,000 at the time of this meeting.
b. Additional Appropriation 2026-27: Sheriff — Vehicle Purchase
Sheriff Derek Fell approached the Council and explained this request was for $25,000 to be appropriated in the
Vehicles line in the LIT Public Safety — County Share Fund to purchase a vehicle. He said the West Terre Haute
Police Department was in the process of a reduction in force and a vehicle has come up for sale. He said this
vehicle, a 2023 Chevy Tahoe, would include equipment and his office valued the vehicle and equipment at
$63,000. He said the West Terre Haute Police Department agreed to sell it to the Vigo County Sheriff's Office
for $30,000. He said the additional $5,000 needed to purchase the vehicle would come from the Sheriff's
Commissary Fund. There was discussion on where the money used to purchase the vehicle would go. Derek
Fell said it would go to the town of West Terre Haute as he understood it.
c. Additional Appropriation 2026-28: Group Homes — House Manager Stipend

Group Homes Executive Director Norm Loudermilk approached the Council and explained this request for
$4,000 to be appropriated from the Group Homes Non-Reverting Fund would fund stipends for House Manager
Positions at the Group Homes. This request was discussed at previous meetings and Norm withdrew a similar
request from the General Fund and was requesting these funds to be appropriated from the Non-Reverting Fund
after discussion at the previous meetings.

Public Comment

There were none.

Appointments

There were none.

Steve noted the Council had been presented with the Sheriff's commissary Ledger for review.

Adjournment

Randy Gentry made a motion to adjourn the meeting. Vicki Weger seconded the motion. The meeting
adjourned at 4:14 p.m.
