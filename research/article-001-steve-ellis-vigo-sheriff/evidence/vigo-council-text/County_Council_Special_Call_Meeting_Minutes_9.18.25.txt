VIGO COUNTY COUNCIL
Special Call Meeting Minutes
Tuesday, September 18, 2025 at 3:30 P.M.
Council Chambers, Vigo County Government Center
Pledge of Allegiance
President David Thompson called the meeting to order at 3:35 p.m.
Calling of the Roll
y Present: Randy Gentry, Nancy Allsup, Steve Ellis, Brad Anderson, David Thompson
Absent: Brenda Wilson, Vicki Weger
First Reading of Binding Units 2026 Budget
a. Terre Haute International Airport
Auditor Larry Hutchings noted the first reading of the Vigo County Solid Waste Management
District and the Terre Haute International Airport’s annual budget needed to happen at this
meeting date due to inadequate time between him filing a notice to taxpayers and the last Council
meeting.
Auditor Larry Hutchings gave the first reading of the 2026 annual budget for the Terre Haute
International Airport for 2026 Annual Budgets as follows: Notice to taxpayers is available online
at www. budgetnotices.in.gov or by calling 888-739-9826. Complete details of budget estimates
by fund and/or department may be seen by visiting the office of this unit of government at 131
Oak St. Terre Haute, IN 47807. Notice is hereby given to taxpayers of the Terre Haute
International Airport that the proper officers of Vigo County will conduct a public hearing on the
year 2026 budget. Following this meeting, any ten or more taxpayers may object to a budget, tax
rate, or tax levy by filing an objection petition with the proper officers of Vigo County not more
than seven days after the hearing. The objection petition must identify the provisions of the
budget, tax rate, or tax levy to which taxpayers object. If a petition is filed, Vigo County shall
adopt with the budget a finding concerning the objections in the petition and testimony
presented. Following the aforementioned hearing, the proper officers of Vigo County will meet
to adopt the following budget. Public hearing date — Tuesday, September 18, 2025 at 5pm at 127
Oak St. Terre Haute, Indiana. The adoption meeting will be Tuesday, October 14, 2025 at 5pm at
127 Oak St. Terre Haute, Indiana. Total budget estimate - $3,393,580 with a maximum estimated
funds to be raised (including appeals and levies exempt from maximum levy limitations -
$2,169,924.
