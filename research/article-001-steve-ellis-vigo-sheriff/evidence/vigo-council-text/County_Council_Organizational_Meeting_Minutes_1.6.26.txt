VIGO COUNTY COUNCIL
Organizational Meeting Minutes
Tuesday, January 6, 2026 at 5:00 P.M.
Council Chambers, Vigo County Government Center
, ;

Pledge of Allegiance

Auditor Larry Hutchings called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m.
Calling of the Roll

Present: Randy Gentry, Nancy Allsup, Brenda Wilson, Steve Ellis, Vicki Weger, Brad Anderson, David
Thompson
Reorganization

a. Election of Officers
Auditor Larry Hutchings opened nominations for the 2026 Vigo County Council President. Vicki Weger
nominated Steve Ellis to be the 2026 Vigo County Council President. Randy Gentry seconded Vicki Weger’s
nomination. There were no further nominations. Vicki Weger made a motion nominations be closed for
President. Randy Gentry seconded the motion. Upon a roll call vote for the nomination of President, voting was
as follows: Randy Gentry — aye; Nancy Allsup — aye; Brenda Wilson — nay; Steve Ellis — aye; Vicki Weger —
aye; Brad Anderson — aye; David Thompson — nay. With a vote of 5-2, Steve Ellis was elected 2025 President
of the Council.
Steve Ellis the opened the floor for nominations for President Pro-Tem. Nancy Allsup made a motion to
nominate Vicki Weger for President Pro-Tem of the Vigo County Council. Randy Gentry Seconded the motion.
Randy Gentry made a motion to close the nominations for President Pro-Tem. Nancy Allsup seconded the
motion. Upon a roll call vote for the nomination of President Pro-Tem, voting was as follows: Randy Gentry —
aye; Nancy Allsup — aye; Brenda Wilson — nay; Steve Ellis — aye; Vicki Weger — aye; Brad Anderson — aye;
David Thompson — nay. With a vote of 5-2, Steve Ellis was elected 2025 President of the Council.

b. Adoption of the 2026 Council Rules
Steve Ellis noted the Council had sixty days to amend the rules. Michael Wright explained the 2026 rules
incorporated the changes made to the 2025 rules at the November County Council meeting. Brad Anderson
made a motion to wait to adopt the 2026 Council Rules. Brenda Wilson seconded the motion. In discussion,
Randy Gentry made a motion to amend the motion on the floor to include: the Council will use the 2025 rules
until adoption of the 2026 rules. Vicki Weger seconded the motion to amend the motion to wait to approve the
2026 council rules. There was discussion the procedure of adopting rules for Council, and this vote. Upon a roll

call vote to amend Brad Anderson’s motion, voting was as follows: Randy Gentry — aye; Nancy Allsup — aye;

Brenda Wilson — aye; Steve Ellis — aye; Vicki Weger — aye; Brad Anderson — aye; David Thompson — nay.
With a vote of 6-1, Brad Anderson’s motion was amended. Then, with a roll call vote to approve Brad
Anderson’s now amended motion, voting was as follows: Randy Gentry — aye; Nancy Allsup — aye; Brenda
Wilson — aye; Steve Ellis — aye; Vicki Weger — aye; Brad Anderson — aye; David Thompson — aye. With a vote
of 7-0, Brad Anderson’s motion was amended.

c. Approving Tentative 2026 Council Meeting Schedule

===== [County_Council_Organizational_Meeting_Minutes_1.6.26] PAGE 2 =====
The tentative meeting schedule was distributed to all Council members prior to this meeting. Randy Gentry
made a motion to approve the 2026 tentative council meeting schedule. Nancy Allsup seconded the motion.
Upon a roll call vote for the pending motion, voting was as follows: Randy Gentry — aye; Nancy Allsup — aye;
Brenda Wilson — aye; Steve Ellis — aye; Vicki Weger — aye; Brad Anderson — aye; David Thompson — aye.
With a vote of 7-0, the motion passed.
Communications from Elected Officials or Agencies of the County
There were none.
Appointments
Steve Ellis said he was going to wait for appointments until the February meeting.
Public Comment
There were none.
Adjournment
Randy Gentry made a motion to adjourn the meeting. Vicki Weger seconded the motion. With a vocal vote, the
meeting was adjourned at 5:12 p.m.
