VIGO COUNTY COUNCIL
Meeting Minutes
Tuesday, May 12, 2026 at 5:00 P.M.
Council Chambers — Vigo County Government Center

Pledge of Allegiance

President Steve Ellis called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m.
Calling of the Roll

Present: Brenda Wilson, Nancy Allsup, David Thompson, Brad Anderson, Randy Gentry, Vicki Weger,
Steve Ellis.
Steve Ellis thanked the Terre Haute Mayor and City Officials for the use of the facilities.
Correcting the Journal of the Preceding Meetings if Needed

April 7, 2026 Sunshine Meeting

April 14, 2026 Meeting
There were no corrections to the April 7, 2026 Sunshine Meeting minutes, David Thompson made a motion to
approve the minutes of the April 7, 2026 Sunshine Meeting minutes as presented. Brenda Wilson seconded the
motion. Upon a roll call vote for the pending motion, voting was as follows: Brenda Wilson — aye; David
Thompson — aye; Nancy Allsup — aye; Brad Anderson — aye; Vicki Weger — aye; Randy Gentry — aye; Steve
Ellis — aye. With a vote of 7-0, the motion passed.
There were no corrections to the April 14, 2026 Meeting minutes, David Thompson made a motion to approve
the minutes of the March 10, 2025 Meeting minutes as presented. Vicki Weger seconded the motion. Nancy
Allsup noted she would abstain since she was not present at the meeting. Upon a roll call vote for the pending
motion, voting was as follows: Brenda Wilson — aye; David Thompson — aye; Nancy Allsup — abstain; Brad
Anderson — aye; Vicki Weger — aye; Randy Gentry — aye; Steve Ellis — aye. With a vote of 6-0, the motion
passed.
Communications from Elected Officials or Agencies of the County
Brenda Wilson provided a statement expressing her displeasure with her opponents in the Indiana Primary for
the State Senate Race and addressed statements made by them and others during the election cycle.
Public Comment
Kevin Southwood of 1638 7" Avenue, Terre Haute approached the Council and requested Council candidates in
the upcoming general election publically note their policies.
Reports from Committees
There were none.
Resolutions and Ordinances Other than Appropriations

a. An Amended Ordinance Adopting the 2026 Salary Schedule and Compensation Policies for

Vigo County Prosecutors and Public Defenders

This request was discussed at the sunshine meeting. Chief Public Defender Gretchen Etling approached the
Council and explained this would add the words “and Public Defenders” to Section 4 of this ordinance and keep

it line with language used in previous years. Randy Gentry made a motion to approve this Amended Ordinance
Adopting the 2026 Salary Schedule and Compensation Policies for Vigo County Prosecutors and Public

Ordinances Relating to Appropriations
a. Additional Appropriation 2026-26: Prosecutor’s Office — Law Enforcement Grants
This request was discussed at the sunshine meeting. There were no further questions. Vicki Weger made a
motion to approve Additional Appropriation 2026-26. Brad Anderson seconded the motion. Upon a roll call
vote for the pending motion, voting was as follows: Brenda Wilson — aye; David Thompson — aye; Nancy
Allsup — aye; Brad Anderson — aye; Vicki Weger — aye; Randy Gentry — aye; Steve Ellis — aye. With a vote of
7-0, the motion passed.
b. Additional Appropriation 2026-27: Sheriff — Vehicle Purchase
This request was discussed at the sunshine meeting. Brad Anderson expressed he thought the price for the
purchase of the West Terre Haute Police Vehicle was a good deal. Brad Anderson made a motion to approve
Additional Appropriation 2026-27. Vicki Weger seconded the motion. Upon a roll call vote for the pending
motion, voting was as follows: Brenda Wilson — aye; David Thompson — aye; Nancy Allsup — aye; Brad
Anderson — aye; Vicki Weger — aye; Randy Gentry — aye; Steve Ellis — aye. With a vote of 7-0, the motion
passed.
c. Additional Appropriation 2026-28: Group Homes — Transportation Vehicle
This request was discussed at the sunshine meeting. There was discussion on if this stipend would be one time
or part of the employees’ regular compensation. Group Homes Executive Director Norm Loudermilk confirmed
the ordinance had been amended to allow this regularly as stated in the ordinance. There was discussion about
the reasoning for a stipend instead of a pay grade change. Norm expressed the additional duties the House
Managers in question were responsible did not warrant a complete pay grade adjustment. There was discussion
about the hierarchy of supervisors at the Group Homes. There was discussion about the Commissioners’ and
Council’s respective responsibilities in the appropriations process. Brenda Wilson made a motion to approve
Additional Appropriation 2026-28. David Thompson seconded the motion. Upon a roll call vote for the pending
motion, voting was as follows: Brenda Wilson — aye; David Thompson — aye; Nancy Allsup — aye; Brad
Anderson — aye; Vicki Weger — aye; Randy Gentry — aye; Steve Ellis — aye. With a vote of 7-0, the motion
passed.
Honorary Resolutions
There were none.
Resolutions Relating to Fiscal Policies of the Council
There were none.
Appointments
Steve Ellis said he was establishing a “financial research committee” and he appointed Cody Francis, Randy
Gentry, Nancy Allsup, Vicki Weger, Tim Drake, and Alex Sacopulos.
Adjournment
Brenda Wilson made a motion to adjourn the meeting. Vicki Weger seconded the motion. With a 7-0 vocal
vote, the meeting was adjourned at 5:35 p.m.
