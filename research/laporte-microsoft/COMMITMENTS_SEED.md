## F · ⚠⚠ ENGINE LESSON — the "fabrication" flag was itself partly wrong. Correcting it.

**What happened.** An earlier research pass flagged an AI summary of the Herald-Dispatch
ordinance article as hallucinated, on the grounds that it invented "a 9-1 Plan Commission
dissent by Jimmy Pressel" plus "quotes from Rita Beatty and Guy DiMartino." I recorded that in
this file as a confirmed second instance of AI fabrication.

**The primary records say otherwise.** County Plan Commission minutes for **4/28/2026** record
a roll call of **8-1 with Jimmy Pressel voting nay**. **Rita Beaty** is the Plan Commission
President. **Guy DiMartino** is the county attorney, who read corrections into the record at the
Commissioners' **5/6/2026** meeting. **All three people are real and correctly placed.** The
summary's actual error appears to be a vote count (9-1 vs 8-1) and possibly a misattributed
quote — not invention.

**Three corrections follow, and the third is the important one:**

1. **This file previously carried a false accusation.** It is retracted here rather than
   quietly deleted, because a correction that hides its own history is worth nothing.
2. **Two AI passes disagreed; only the primary record settled it.** Neither pass was reliable
   alone — including the one that sounded most rigorous.
3. **A false-positive fabrication flag is its own failure mode.** We were so primed to catch
   hallucination that we manufactured one, and had we published it we would have accused a real
   newspaper of inventing real people. **An engine that cries wolf about AI error is not safer
   than one that ignores it — it is differently wrong, and it burns the credibility that the
   caution was meant to protect.**

**What survives unchanged:** the underlying rule is still right, and the Vigo case (a summarizer
producing three different budget figures from one article) is still a confirmed fabrication.

> **Prose from an AI summarizer is not evidence — and neither is an AI's accusation that
> another AI fabricated something. Both resolve only against the sealed artifact.**

Schema consequence: `verification_record` is versioned precisely so a finding like this can be
**superseded rather than erased**, and `evidence.extracted_by` / `rendition.is_machine_text`
remain the enforcement points. Add to the engine spec: **a fabrication finding is itself a claim
and must clear the same evidence bar as any other.**
